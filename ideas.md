# A’s n B Mobile Detailing — Brand-Led Website Revision

## Chosen Direction: High-Gloss Mobile Service Deck

### Design Movement

The site is redesigned as a **high-gloss automotive service deck**. It takes the strong hierarchy and metallic character of the supplied sales materials, then adapts them into a more breathable, conversion-oriented web experience.

### Core Principles

1. **Make the mobile value obvious.** The phone number, “We Come to You,” and booking actions remain visible and direct.
2. **Treat blue, gold, and black as the primary identity.** Navy establishes the professional field, electric blue adds automotive energy, and metallic gold carries premium emphasis.
3. **Use structure over clutter.** Gold rules, package cards, compact service tiles, and generous breathing room organize the service menu without duplicating flyer density.
4. **Use human confidence.** The voice stays direct, professional, and approachable: premium results without inflated claims.

### Color Philosophy

Deep navy **#031538** replaces generic black as the principal brand surface. Royal/electric blue **#0754C7–#0875ED** communicates clarity and automotive performance. Metallic gold **#9A6506–#F7CF58** signals premium care and directs attention to pricing and primary actions. Pink **#E95591** is reserved for rare finishing accents—such as a small heart or rule—not an everyday site color.

### Layout Paradigm

The homepage follows a **service-deck cadence**: a hero that establishes brand and contact, a compact proof section, a structured service matrix, price markers, package cards, a mobile-service process, answers, and a decisive booking panel.

### Signature Elements

1. Metallic gold rules, thin borders, and gradient buttons.
2. Electric-blue panel illumination and automotive arc lines.
3. Gold starbursts used as controlled high-gloss finishing marks.

### Interaction Philosophy

The strongest paths are direct call and email actions. Secondary navigation moves visitors through the service deck. Package cards rise subtly on hover; buttons provide immediate compression feedback.

### Typography System

**Bebas Neue** handles large, uppercase automotive headlines and pricing. **Cinzel** supplies an established Roman-capitals feel for small brand labels. **DM Sans** keeps service descriptions and questions accessible on every device.

### Brand Essence

**A’s n B Mobile Detailing delivers professional automotive care at the customer’s location, combining high-gloss results with straightforward convenience.**

**Personality:** professional, energetic, detail-oriented.

### Brand Voice

Headlines make a practical promise; microcopy makes booking clear. Core recurring lines are “Professional Detailing. Premium Results.” and “We Come to You!”

### Logo System

The supplied blue-and-gold circular logo is the primary large-format mark. It is used at a readable size in the header, hero context, and footer. Its internal script remains the only highly decorative typography on the page.
