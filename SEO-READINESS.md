# Local Search & AI Discovery Readiness

## What is already in the website

The finished homepage uses one descriptive H1, clear service-level headings, location-intent prompts in the quote form, a visible question-and-answer section, crawlable service copy, a concise title and meta description, Open Graph metadata, robots directives, and JSON-LD for the website, services, and FAQ content. This gives search systems and answer engines a plain-language explanation of Ross Mobile Detailing’s offering without making unsupported geographic or service claims.

## Before publishing

The business owner should replace the placeholder canonical website domain in the JSON-LD, connect the quote form to Ross’s lead inbox or CRM, and add the confirmed business phone number, service-area towns or ZIP codes, operating hours, and Google Business Profile link. After those details are confirmed, add them consistently to the footer, the Business Profile, and the LocalBusiness JSON-LD configuration.

| Priority | Action | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Verify and fully complete the Google Business Profile. | Google states that complete and accurate information can help a business show for relevant local searches. [1] |
| 2 | Add the real service area, phone, hours, and customer-facing address policy. | Google’s LocalBusiness documentation identifies `address` and `name` as required properties, with details such as phone, URL, hours, and geo coordinates recommended where applicable. [2] |
| 3 | Keep the service pages and profile aligned. | Google describes local ranking as driven mainly by relevance, distance, and prominence; consistent service and location information helps establish relevance. [1] |
| 4 | Ask customers for honest reviews and reply to them. | Google notes that positive reviews and helpful responses can help a business stand out. Do not create or add reviews that do not originate from actual customers. [1] |
| 5 | Publish real project photography periodically. | Google recommends photos and videos as a way to show what the business offers and tell its story. [1] |

## Notes on terminology

“GEO” and “AIO” are treated here as **generative-engine and answer-engine readiness**, not as a separate guaranteed ranking channel. The practical approach is factual, accessible service copy; direct answers to common questions; accurate business details; and standards-based structured data. Structured data helps Google understand page content but does not guarantee a specific rich result. [2]
